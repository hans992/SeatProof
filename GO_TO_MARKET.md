# Go-to-market and first 20 target criteria

> Historical record — superseded on 28 October 2026 by [COMMERCIAL_VERDICT.md](COMMERCIAL_VERDICT.md). Commercial development is stopped; no compelling advantage over the four compared products was established. Earlier pilot, pricing, outreach and feature proposals below are inactive hypotheses, not the current recommendation.

## Initial offer

Sell a scoped diagnostic before a subscription: one month, two exports, reviewed quantity-exception packet, €249 proposed fixed fee. Explain what is excluded and show the known false-positive case. Ask for a second monthly run only after the buyer experiences useful findings with acceptable setup effort. A €149/month recurring pilot is an unvalidated proposal, not a market price.

The first channel hypothesis is independent MSP bookkeepers and PSA implementation consultants who can bring an existing trusted relationship. Direct MSP-owner discovery is the second. Do not assume public community visibility is a sales channel. Ask moderators before promotion; obtain explicit authorization before any outreach. No contacts were made in this project.

## Twenty prospecting slots, expressed as company criteria

These are **target-company criteria**, not twenty identified leads or claims that twenty companies qualify. Use public company websites and business directories; record company name, website, public service evidence, source URL, observation date and qualification outcome. Do not collect personal email addresses or infer private billing pain from an employee's profile.

Public discovery starting points verified on 28 October 2026: [Microsoft partner directories](https://microsoftpartners.microsoft.com/abs/Partner-Directories/) and [Microsoft Azure partners](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/partners/). These are channel discovery sources, not evidence that listed companies are small or dissatisfied. A candidate's own website must confirm relevant services. Avoid targeting large Azure specialists simply because they appear in the directory.

Common disqualifiers: happy existing reconciliation workflow; no canonical-ID mapping; most revenue bundled/metered; no permission to provide anonymized records; need immediate write-back; unwillingness to pay for a diagnostic; onboarding estimated above two hours without a reusable mapping.

## Experiment sequence

Week 1 after explicit outreach authorization: identify twenty company candidates against these slots; aim for five workflow conversations, not a claimed response rate. Use the interview protocol, avoid a leading pitch and gather anonymized examples with consent. Track reasons for refusals.

Week 2: offer ten qualified buyers the same bounded paid diagnostic. Two paid acceptances is the proposed continuation threshold. Zero acceptances is a stop signal. Do not redefine qualification after seeing results.

Weeks 3–4: time onboarding and review, compare with observed manual baseline, ask for a paid second billing cycle. Three voluntary repeats are a proposed retention gate. If buyers request native PSA integration before paying, compare a setup-service business against building software.

## Funnel and economics to measure

Track qualified businesses, conversations, usable export pairs, paid diagnostics, onboarding minutes, manual-review minutes, incorrect proposals, actionable verified findings, and second-cycle renewals. Record price objections verbatim only with permission. No conversion rates, CAC, LTV or payback estimates are presented as facts before observations exist.
