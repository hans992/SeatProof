# Preservation status and conditions for reconsideration

**Commercial development stopped — 28 October 2026.** The [final assessment](COMMERCIAL_VERDICT.md) supersedes the previous pilot and feature roadmap. No compelling customer advantage over CloudOlive, Gradient Reconcile, BillRecon or BillingReconcile was established.

## Current disposition

- Preserve the working implementation, synthetic examples, tests, documented failures and original Git history in [hans992/SeatProof](https://github.com/hans992/SeatProof).
- Keep the demo available for engineering study and portfolio review. Follow [README.md](README.md) and [DEMO.md](DEMO.md).
- Treat earlier pricing, outreach and interview materials as inactive historical drafts. There is no current sales offer, outreach campaign, paid pilot or feature roadmap.
- Keep [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and [TEST_RESULTS.md](TEST_RESULTS.md) limitations visible. Preservation does not imply production readiness or a maintenance commitment.

## Conditions for reopening the commercial question

Require an observed customer workflow where an existing product demonstrably fails, SeatProof succeeds on the same records, and the customer is willing to pay for that difference. Assess preparation effort, review accuracy and total cost against the actual alternative. Offline operation and evidence hashes alone are not proof of demand.

No implementation, prospect contact, purchase or deployment is authorized by this document. The previous roadmap remains available in Git history.
