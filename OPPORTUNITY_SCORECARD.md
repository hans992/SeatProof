# Adversarial scorecard

Dated 2026-10-28. Scores are analyst judgments on 0–5, not measured market statistics. Weighted total = sum(score / 5 × weight). Penalties are subtracted as explicit points; overlapping risks can compound. No candidate passes a commercial validation gate.

Weights: Recurring pain 25%; Spending 15%; Differentiation 20%; Reachability 15%; Feasibility/cost 10%; Revenue/expansion 10%; Portfolio 5%.

Penalty order: Unproven willingness to pay; Equivalent incumbents; Enterprise sales cycle; Legal exposure; Unavailable data; Onboarding; Integration cost; Inference cost; Speculative trend.

## Sensitivity

MSP's lead survives either alone but does not imply readiness to launch: its net remains below 50. Raising its equivalent-incumbent penalty another ten points still leaves a prototype rationale, not commercial proof. Paid-pilot evidence must replace these judgments.
